# SaaSCRM FAQ

Common questions about the SaaSCRM Next.js CRM template.

- **What do I get?** Full React + Next.js source, 60+ CRM screens, shadcn/ui, marketing landing, agentic AI chatbot, theme customizer, Zod schemas, Design.md, and lifetime updates.
- **Commercial use?** Yes — MIT license for your SaaS or client work.
- **Custom builds?** Yes. Book a call from https://saascrm.site/contact.
- **Do you have an agency CRM?** Yes. Read https://saascrm.site/blog/agency-crm and demo https://saascrm.site/templates/agency.
- **Where are the CRM guides?** https://saascrm.site/blog — including https://saascrm.site/blog/best-crm-for-small-businesses-and-startups, https://saascrm.site/blog/why-your-business-needs-a-crm, and https://saascrm.site/blog/six-crm-templates-you-can-demo-today.
- **Live demo?** https://saascrm.site/demo
- **Stack?** Next.js 12, React 17, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Recharts, Zod.

Full answers: https://saascrm.site/faq
