## What is the AI Builder?

The AI Builder lives at [/blueprint](https://saascrm.site/blueprint). Think of it as a CRM architect in your browser.

You tell it:

- Business type (SaaS, agency, recruiting, real estate, and more)
- Team size
- Backend plan (prototype, Supabase, Firebase, existing API…)
- Launch target (prototype, MVP, or production)
- Features you need — Leads, Pipeline, Invoices, CMS, Analytics, and so on
- A short note on what you’re building

It returns a blueprint you can actually use — not a vague “AI summary.”

## What does it provide?

After you generate a plan, you get:

1. **Overview** — what’s already covered by the template vs what needs custom work  
2. **Page map** — the CRM screens you should ship first  
3. **Recommended stack** — frontend and backend direction that fits your answers  
4. **Timeline** — realistic estimates for prototype, frontend, backend, and production  
5. **Cursor prompts** — copy-paste prompts tailored to the SaaSCRM project rules  
6. **Build CRM** — a path to start from the template (or generate a small free starter)

In short: less guessing, more shipping.

## How to use it (simple)

1. Open **[Plan your CRM](https://saascrm.site/blueprint)**  
2. Pick your business type, team size, backend, and target  
3. Tap the modules you need  
4. Write one sentence about what you’re building  
5. Hit **Generate free CRM blueprint**  
6. Review the tabs — especially **Pages**, **Timeline**, and **Cursor prompts**

That’s it. You can run a few plans per hour for free and refine as you go.
