## Why templates?

Building a CRM from a blank Next.js app is slow in the boring ways:

- Sidebar + routing + empty states for every module
- Tables, filters, detail pages, and seed data that actually look real
- Theme tokens so dark mode and rebrands don’t break mid-ship

**Templates skip that setup.** You pick a vertical that already matches your workflow, demo it, then customize instead of inventing the admin layer from scratch.

That’s the whole idea behind SaaSCRM:

- **Small verticals** → prove the UI for your niche fast  
- **Full kit** → 60+ screens when you need sales, ops, finance, CMS, and system in one product

## How to use each one

Same playbook for every template:

1. Open the **[templates gallery](https://saascrm.site/templates)** and click a card  
2. Hit **View live demo** — it loads that vertical inside the SaaSCRM shell  
3. Click around the sidebar (that *is* the product shape)  
4. When you’re ready to own the code, grab the starter / pack from the template page or upgrade to the full kit  

### Indie Stripe Billing

Best if you live in Stripe Dashboard tabs. Demo → swap seed customers / plans → wire real Stripe later. Home lands on revenue; dig into failed charges and subscriptions.

### Agency CRM

Best for studios and boutique agencies. Walk **Leads → Pipeline → Invoices → Projects**, then rename stages to match how you actually close and deliver.

### Freelancer CRM

Best for solo operators. Keep **Clients / Invoices / Payments / Tasks** as the daily loop. Replace seed clients with your roster and ship.

### Creator CMS

Best for writers and content teams. Use **CMS + Analytics + Users**, then point blog / newsletter rows at your real calendar or email tool.

### Digital Store CRM

Best for digital product sellers. Start on **Products**, then Orders → Invoices → Payments. Badge SKUs as ebook / PDF / icon pack as you grow.

### Ecommerce CMS

Best for DTC / ops-heavy stores. Run **Products → Inventory → Orders → Shipping**, track raw materials, and keep marketing in CMS + Scheduler without leaving the admin.
