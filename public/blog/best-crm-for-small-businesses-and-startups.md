## What “best” means when you’re small

A good [best crm for startups](https://saascrm.site/templates) is not the one with 400 features. It’s the one your team will open every morning:

- **Leads and contacts** in one place (not three Google Sheets)
- A **pipeline** you can see without a 30-minute standup
- Invoices, tasks, and follow-ups next to the deal — not in another tab
- Source code you can change when the business changes

That’s why a lot of founders bounce between a [free open-source crm](https://saascrm.site/templates) they have to self-host, and a hosted giant they can’t customize.

## Frappe CRM alternative vs Twenty CRM alternative

Two names come up a lot in “open source CRM” threads:

- **Frappe CRM** — excellent if you already live in the Frappe/ERPNext world. Python, desk UI, self-host. Heavy if you just wanted a React admin for a 4-person SaaS.
- **Twenty CRM** — a modern [free open-source crm](https://saascrm.site/templates). Beautiful, but you still run infra, upgrades, and the “we need invoices + CMS + a custom module by Friday” problem.

If your team ships in **Next.js**, a [frappe CRM alternative](https://saascrm.site/templates) and a [twenty CRM alternative](https://saascrm.site/demo) should feel like *your* codebase: Tailwind, shadcn, Cursor prompts, not a second framework to learn.

SaaSCRM is that shape — a production CRM/admin/CMS kit you customize, not another hosted CRM you rent.
