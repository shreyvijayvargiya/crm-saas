## Why do we need a CRM?

The most basic question we always ask clients or pitch leads is: *Why do we need a CRM?*

The obvious answer is to manage work in one platform — a more centralised system, less prone to error, and a more secure, modern way of doing business online is to have an agency CRM.

But we give something different as our answer. We tell people that CRM helps you keep the business on track, manage users, view analytics, check SEO blogs, track payments, create invoices, and more. This entire process creates discipline among teams and founders — giving an upper edge to teams that actually use CRM.

> CRM is not a need — it's an answer to your future multiple-tabs distraction.

## Why SAAS CRM and how it can help?

SAAS CRM is the **CRM, admin, and CMS starter kit** template built on the latest technology stack. It sits underneath your existing online business in the background, helping you quietly manage and run the entire business.

- SAAS CRM provides **60+ pages** for Admin, CRM, and CMS — Leads, Proposals, Invoice, Payments, Sales, Analytics, and more
- SAAS CRM provides an **AI agent chatbot** that can answer queries and perform tasks such as creating an invoice
- SAAS CRM is **customisable** and can integrate with Stripe, PayPal, Supabase, Polar, Lemon Squeezy, Notion, Linear, Google Drive, Google Analytics, PostHog, Slack, and more

## How to build my CRM in days?

It's not that hard. We provide technical support and email support to either add a CRM to your existing business or build a custom one — it depends on the need.

- SAAS CRM template provides a **Next.js, React, shadcn/ui, Tailwind** stack that is easy to customise, edit, and responsive by default with dark/light theme support
- SAAS CRM template provides **database API schemas** for Prisma, SQL, NoSQL, MongoDB, SQLite, Supabase, and Firebase
- SAAS CRM template provides **deployment steps** and documentation so you can start quickly and build an advanced CRM on your own

### Building CRM using SAAS CRM in 3 steps

1. **Get the SAAS CRM** — download the zip and unzip the file
2. **Install packages** with `npm install`, then run the repository locally
3. **Edit** the files, pages, and tables — then deploy or push to GitHub

In just a few steps, your CRM is ready. Integrating with tools and APIs comes next.
