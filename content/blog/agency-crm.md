## What is an agency CRM?

An **agency CRM** is a customer and operations system designed for agencies — not a generic sales database with a new label.

In a generic CRM you store people and move deals. In **CRM for agencies**, those people become paying clients, those clients own projects, those projects have tasks and deliverables, and those deliverables become invoices and collected payments. That is **agency client management software**: one record graph, not five subscriptions.

If you are still asking *why bother*, start with [why your business needs a CRM](https://saascrm.site/blog/why-your-business-needs-a-crm). The agency version of that answer is specific: **visibility across sales and delivery**.

## An agency rarely has only one workflow

A new lead becomes a proposal.

The proposal becomes a client.

The client creates a project.

The project creates deliverables.

The deliverables create invoices.

The invoice creates revenue.

But many agencies manage these stages in completely different tools — a form here, a sheet there, [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/invoices/) or a PDF for billing, WhatsApp for follow-ups, and a project board that never talks to the pipeline.

That is where an **agency CRM** becomes useful. Indian agencies especially live in WhatsApp threads; the CRM has to be the system of record so a chat does not become the only place a client exists.

## How to run leads, clients, projects, and invoices in one system

1. **Capture leads** in one inbox — forms, referrals, WhatsApp handoff — not a shared sheet.
2. **Move the pipeline** (qualification → proposal → won) so nothing sits in “we’ll follow up.”
3. **Convert the won deal** into a client record. Do not start a second spreadsheet.
4. **Open a project** with tasks, owners, and due dates under that client.
5. **Invoice from the same record** when deliverables ship; record payments when they land.
6. **Ask the copilot** for unpaid invoices, overdue projects, and quiet leads.
